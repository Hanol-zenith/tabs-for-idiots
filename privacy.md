# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Tabsy for Ukes handles information when you use our mobile application.

## Summary

The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. Any data the app stores is kept locally on your device.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** Specifically:

- **No account required.** You can use the app without signing up or providing any personal details.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- **No crash reporting.** We do not use crash-reporting services that transmit device or usage data.

## Local Storage

The app allows you to save favorite songs for quick access. This information is stored **locally on your device** using standard iOS storage mechanisms. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app removes this data.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the app collects no personal information at all, this applies universally regardless of user age.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect any personal data to begin with.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
